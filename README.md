# Government Grant Indirect Cost Recovery

Recover allowable indirect costs through correct rate bases, exclusions, carryforwards, and retroactive adjustments.

**Primary buyer:** Universities, nonprofits, and government contractors. **Evidence:** grant agreements, approved rates, direct costs, cost pools, exclusions, subawards, drawdowns, invoices, rate adjustments, and reimbursements.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Award agreement library
- Indirect rate agreement
- Direct cost ingestion
- Modified total direct cost base
- Capital expenditure exclusion
- Subaward threshold exclusion
- Participant support exclusion
- Indirect cost calculation
- Provisional-to-final adjustment
- Carryforward calculation
- Award ceiling control
- Drawdown reconciliation
- Sponsor adjustment request
- Cash recovery tracking
- Award rate analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4690>. API: `5690`.

Administrator: `runtime-admin@example.com` / `LocalDemo!2026`. Operator and reviewer credential buttons are available on the login page.
